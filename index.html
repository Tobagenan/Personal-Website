About Me

I am a plastics engineer who enjoys turning engineering concepts into practical, real-world solutions. My interests include manufacturing, product development, and finding ways to make production processes more efficient and reliable.

Through my projects, I explore different aspects of engineering, from developing assembly line concepts to creating bills of materials and manufacturing documentation. I am particularly interested in how thoughtful design and process planning can improve the way products are made.

This portfolio is a collection of my engineering work, the skills I have developed, and the problems I have worked to solve. I am always looking to learn, take on new challenges, and find better ways to bring ideas to life.
